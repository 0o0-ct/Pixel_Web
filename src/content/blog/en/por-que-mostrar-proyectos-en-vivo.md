At Design Web we have a non-negotiable rule: **if a project is in our showcase, it is published and running.** No mockups, no simulated demos.

It started as a way to stand out… and ended up being our best sales tool.

## The most honest social proof

A screenshot tells you what a website looks like. A live link tells you how it actually works:

- **Real speed**: you load it on your own device and feel it.
- **Real interaction**: you browse the menu, you test the form.
- **Real durability**: if the project is still online months later, something is being done right.

When a potential client opens one of your sites at 2 AM from their phone, trust builds itself. No sales pitch comes close.

## How we do it at Design Web

1. **Every public project is published on its real domain.** If the site is in development, we use a temporary URL until launch.
2. **We label the status.** Each card in our showcase shows whether the site is *live*.
3. **We keep it current.** When a client renews or improves their site, the showcase version updates too.

This also keeps us honest with ourselves: we cannot promise performance we do not deliver, because it is one click away from being checked.

## What about you?

If you sell digital services or products, ask yourself: **what can your customer see live before buying?**

- A demo of your product.
- A case study with real data.
- Your own process documented in public.

What can be verified, convinces. What is only told, is doubted.
